Functional Requirements
The functional requirements define what the system should do. For the cooperative management system (Adashe app), the key functional requirements include:
1.	Member Management
o	Registration of members with unique profiles.
o	Role-based access for administrators and members.
2.	Contribution Management
o	Recording and tracking of member contributions.
o	Notifications/reminders for upcoming contributions.
3.	Loan Management
o	Loan application, approval, and disbursement.
o	Tracking of repayment schedules.
4.	Savings & Payouts
o	Monitoring group savings.
o	Automated calculation of payouts/dividends.
5.	Financial Transparency
o	Real-time reporting of contributions and expenditures.
o	Secure digital receipts for every transaction.
6.	Accountability & Fraud Prevention
o	Audit logs of all transactions.
o	Verification features to prevent unauthorized changes.
7.	Communication & Engagement
o	In-app messaging and announcements.
o	Notifications for meetings and updates.
8.	Gamification Features (Optional)
o	Contribution scoring system to encourage timely payments.
o	Rewards or recognition for consistent contributors.

 Non-Functional Requirements
Non-functional requirements define the qualities and constraints of the system.
1.	Performance
o	The system should handle multiple users concurrently without lag.
2.	Scalability
o	The system must support an increasing number of members and cooperatives.
3.	Security
o	Data encryption, authentication, and role-based authorization.
o	Secure payment integration.
4.	Usability
o	Simple, intuitive user interface accessible to members with varying levels of digital literacy.
5.	Reliability & Availability
o	24/7 system availability with minimal downtime.
o	Regular data backups.
6.	Portability
o	Accessible via mobile devices (Android/iOS) and web browsers.
7.	Maintainability
o	Modular system architecture for easy updates and feature additions.
